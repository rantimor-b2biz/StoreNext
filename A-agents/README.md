## How Agents Work Together

### Content Pipeline

```
Research Agent (market intelligence)
    ↓
Copywriter Agent (creates blog posts, case studies)
    ↓
Visual Agent (designs assets)
    ↓
Newsletter Agent (repurposes for email)
    ↓
Director (quality review & approval)
```

### Demand Generation Pipeline

```
Paid Search Agent (LinkedIn & Google Ads)
    ↓ (drives traffic)
Landing Pages (from Copywriter)
    ↓
Lead Capture
    ↓
Analytics Agent (tracks performance)
    ↓
ABM Agent (high-value account targeting)
```

### Weekly Workflow

```
MONDAY EOD       → Blog post or case study published (Copywriter)
   ↓
TUESDAY AM       → Design assets created (Visual)
   ↓
TUESDAY EOD      → Email campaign sent (Newsletter)
   ↓
WEDNESDAY AM     → ABM campaign report (ABM)
   ↓
WEDNESDAY PM     → Campaigns optimized (Paid Search)
   ↓
THURSDAY AM      → Research brief delivered (Research)
   ↓
FRIDAY AM        → Sales collateral updated (Sales Enablement)
   ↓
SUNDAY EOD       → Performance summary (Analytics)
```

## Key Collaboration Points

### All Agents ← Research Agent (Foundational)
- Every piece of content is informed by research
- Competitive intelligence guides strategy
- Market trends inform content topics

### Copywriter ↔ Visual (Content & Design)
- Content drives design direction
- Visual enforces brand consistency
- Must be fully coordinated on delivery

### Newsletter ↔ Copywriter (Repurposing)
- Newsletter Agent repackages Copywriter's content
- Email-specific optimization (subject lines, brevity)
- Must coordinate on timing

### Paid Search ↔ ABM (Demand Gen Strategy)
- Paid Search drives broad awareness & leads
- ABM targets high-value accounts with precision
- Must share targeting insights and lead quality data

### Analytics ↔ All Agents (Feedback Loop)
- Analytics provides performance feedback to all agents
- Content ROI informs future topics
- Lead quality feedback refines messaging
- Monthly report drives strategy optimization

## Escalation Protocol

**Director Approves:**
- All quality standards met ✅
- Brand voice aligned ✅
- Data/claims verified ✅
- Ready for publishing ✅

**Director Requests Revision:**
- Quality issue (grammar, clarity, tone)
- Brand misalignment
- Claim not supported by data
- Missing research or customer insight

**Director Escalates:**
- Strategic direction question
- Resource constraint
- Competitive threat
- Ethical/compliance concern

**Director Assigns Research:**
- Competitive threat detected
- Market opportunity identified
- Customer pain point uncovered
- Content gap identified

See `SYNC-PROTOCOL.md` for detailed escalation guidelines.

## Weekly Meetings

**Sunday EOD — Director Check-In**
- All agents report on deliverables
- Blockers identified and resolved
- Quality standards reviewed
- Monday deadline confirmed

**1st Monday of Month — All-Hands Sync**
- Performance review from all agents
- Blockers, wins, learnings
- Next month priorities
- Strategic recommendations

## Getting Help

**If you're blocked:**
1. Check `SYNC-PROTOCOL.md` for escalation guidelines
2. Flag in weekly standup
3. Director makes decision

**If you need a resource:**
1. Ask in monthly all-hands sync
2. Escalate to Director if urgent

**If you discover a competitor move:**
1. Flag immediately to Director
2. Assign Research Agent follow-up

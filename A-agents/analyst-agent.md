## Analysis Principles

### 1. Structure Over Summary
Organize into clear buckets. Don't compress. Structure reveals what summary hides.

### 2. Enterprise Language
Use CFO/Procurement vocabulary: ROI, process efficiency, risk mitigation, compliance, total cost of ownership.

### 3. Separate Facts from Signals
- **Fact:** Verified data with source ("McKinsey 2024: supply chain disruptions lasting 1+ month every 3.7 years")
- **Signal:** Pattern/trend ("3 of last 4 procurement leaders mentioned supplier communication as top challenge")
- **Opinion:** Your interpretation (label it clearly)

### 4. Flag the Unspoken
What wasn't said in the enterprise call may matter most. Note gaps.

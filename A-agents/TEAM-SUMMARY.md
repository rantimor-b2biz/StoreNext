## 🎯 Agent Roles (Quick)

| Agent | Primary Focus | Key Deliverable | Frequency |

| **Research** | Market intelligence, competitive analysis | Weekly research brief | Weekly |
| **Copywriter** | Blog, case studies, sales copy, website | Blog posts + case studies | 2–4x/week |
| **Paid Search** | LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads, lead gen | Campaign setup & optimization | Ongoing |

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Pipeline KPIs
- ✅ Qualified Enterprise leads per month
- ✅ Lead quality score (7+/10)
- ✅ Sales conversion rate from marketing leads
- ✅ Estimated pipeline value

### Content KPIs
- ✅ Blog post engagement (views, time on page)
- ✅ Case study downloads
- ✅ Email open rate (25–35%)
- ✅ Email click rate (3–5%)

### Paid KPIs
- ✅ Cost per lead (target: [Director sets])
- ✅ LinkedIn ads CTR (3–5%)
- ✅ Campaign ROI (positive within 30 days)
- ✅ Monthly leads from paid

### ABM KPIs
- ✅ Account engagement rate
- ✅ Meetings scheduled from ABM
- ✅ Pipeline value from targeted accounts
- ✅ Deal velocity (faster than non-ABM)

### Brand KPIs
- ✅ Website traffic (monthly)
- ✅ LinkedIn followers growth
- ✅ Brand search volume
- ✅ Thought leadership mentions

## 🎨 Visual Elements

### Typography

**Headlines (Main Problem Statement)**
- Font: Inter Bold or similar sans-serif
- Size: 48pt (scalable, but large enough to read at 1:1 LinkedIn feed)
- Color: White (#FFFFFF)
- Line-height: 1.2 (compact, impactful)
- Text: Center aligned
- Example text: "Email chaos at Enterprise scale"

**Secondary Text (Stat)**
- Font: Inter Bold
- Size: 36pt
- Color: Turquoise (#7CCBC3)
- Example: "500+ Suppliers"

**Tagline (Bottom)**
- Font: Inter Regular
- Size: 18pt
- Color: White (#FFFFFF)
- Example: "Rethinking Procurement at Scale"

### Visual Design Elements

**Background**
- Primary: Purple (#3C2C4C)
- Effect: Subtle gradient (darker at edges, slightly lighter in center)
- Purpose: Creates depth, draws focus to text

**Accent Line**
- Color: Red (#E85252)
- Style: Solid horizontal line
- Placement: Above tagline (3-5px height)
- Purpose: Breaks monotony, directs attention

**Network/Connection Graphics**
- Style: Subtle, minimal line graphics
- Purpose: Suggest supply chain interconnection (not heavy or distracting)
- Placement: Background, light opacity (15-20%)
- Color: Turquoise (#7CCBC3) at 20% opacity
- Examples: Connecting nodes, flow arrows, supply chain visualization

**Logo**
- Size: 60-80px width
- Placement: Bottom right corner (20px margin)
- Style: Full color StoreNext logo on dark background
- Ensure: Legible at small sizes

## ✅ Design Quality Checklist

### Visual Standards
- [ ] Purple (#3C2C4C) background correct shade
- [ ] Turquoise (#7CCBC3) text/accents readable
- [ ] Red (#E85252) accent line visible but not overwhelming
- [ ] White text (#FFFFFF) has maximum contrast against purple
- [ ] No text is pixelated or blurry at full size
- [ ] Logo is clear and properly placed

### Brand Alignment
- [ ] Uses StoreNext color palette exclusively
- [ ] Professional tone (not playful or cartoonish)
- [ ] Minimal imagery (don't overcrowd with graphics)
- [ ] Typography is clean and readable
- [ ] No gradients or effects that distort brand colors
- [ ] Logo placement follows brand guidelines

### Readability & Performance
- [ ] Text readable at thumbnail size (LinkedIn feed)
- [ ] Contrast ratio minimum 4.5:1 (accessibility)
- [ ] File size optimized (under 5MB)
- [ ] No gradients that compress poorly
- [ ] Font rendering clean on all browsers

### LinkedIn-Specific
- [ ] Dimensions exactly 1200×628px
- [ ] No extra margins or padding
- [ ] Text placement avoids edges (safe zone: 50px from edge)
- [ ] Color-coded to stand out in feed
- [ ] CTA (post copy) pairs well with this visual

---

## 🎯 What NOT to Do

❌ **Avoid These:**
- Stock photos or generic imagery
- Too many colors (stick to purple, turquoise, red, white only)
- Blurry or low-resolution graphics
- Cartoonish illustrations or emojis
- Text overlays that are hard to read
- Busy backgrounds that distract from text
- Too much going on (less is more)
- 3D effects or shadows that look cheap

---

## 📊 Design Variations Approved

If you want to explore variations:
1. **Gradient Background:** Light purple → Dark purple (maintain contrast)
2. **Network Graphics:** More prominent or more subtle (test both)
3. **Typography:** Arial/Segoe instead of Inter (if Inter not available)
4. **Layout:** Center vs. Left-aligned text (test both)

**But keep the core:**
- Purple + Turquoise + Red color scheme
- "500+ Suppliers" stat
- "Email chaos at Enterprise scale" problem statement
- Clean, professional aesthetic

Most enterprises can pass an annual supplier audit. Very few can answer an auditor's question on any given Tuesday.

That gap is becoming a problem.

A wave of due-diligence regulation is moving supplier documentation onto the CFO's ongoing risk agenda.

CSRD and CSDDD-style regimes now treat supplier evidence as continuous, not annual.

Kodiak Hub's 2026 outlook confirms it. Continuous supplier risk monitoring is replacing periodic reviews as the baseline expectation.

Here is why most enterprises struggle to meet it.

Supplier documentation does not live in a continuous system. It lives in email threads, shared drives, and individual inboxes.

So when an auditor asks for a supplier's current certification status, the answer requires manual reconstruction.

That is not a failure yet. It is a risk waiting for the wrong audit at the wrong time.

This is exactly where a centralized supplier document and communication layer becomes critical.

Done well, it delivers:

- One system of record for contracts, certifications, and correspondence
- Every document version and message timestamped with a clear owner
- Complete supplier trails retrievable in minutes, connected to the ERP

The insight: continuous compliance is not a faster audit cycle. It is a different data model.

A policy without a timestamped record no longer satisfies the bar.

Could your team produce a complete, audit-ready supplier record on demand today?

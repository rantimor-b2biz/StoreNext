**Format:** Data/stat + financial insight

Undocumented open commitments.
A finance team reacting to the past, not managing the future.

The problem does not start in accounting. It starts in supplier communication.

With a centralized supplier portal, every order connects to an invoice. Every invoice connects to a payment.

300+ Enterprise organizations. 2 million transactions per day. Everything documented.

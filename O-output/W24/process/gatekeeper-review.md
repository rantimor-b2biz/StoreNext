## SELECTION DECISION

**SELECTED: Version A (Israeli Regulatory Urgency)**

**Rationale:**
1. **Timeliness is irreplaceable.** The June 1 regulation effective date will lose urgency within weeks. Version A must be published now or the "2 days ago" hook becomes "X weeks ago" — losing its power entirely.
2. **Competitive exclusivity.** No global procurement platform (Basware, Coupa, Tradeshift) can make this Israeli regulatory connection. This is StoreNext's exclusive angle in the market this week.
3. **Compliance urgency > efficiency argument.** For a CFO, "you may not be able to deduct VAT" is a more immediate concern than "your team is slow at processing invoices." Risk language activates faster than efficiency language at the CFO level.
4. **Version B is strong but evergreen.** The cash flow blind spot argument is always true. It can be used in a future campaign week. Version A cannot wait.

**Version B recommendation:** Hold for use in a future campaign week (e.g., Week 9 or Week 10 — Compliance & Integration focus weeks). The 17.4 vs. 3.1 days stat will remain relevant.

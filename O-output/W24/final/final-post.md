As of June 1, 2026, every invoice above ₪5,000 in Israel requires a real-time allocation number.

That took effect two days ago.

An organization with 500 active suppliers receives dozens of invoices every day. If your approval process is still email-based, every invoice without a valid allocation number cannot be used for VAT deduction.

This is not an IT problem. It is a financial exposure.

Managing allocation numbers across multiple suppliers, simultaneously, by email, is not operationally possible.

300+ Enterprise organizations in Israel have already moved to a centralized supplier portal: the supplier submits, the system validates, the ERP receives — automatically.

2 million transactions per day. All documented. All compliant.

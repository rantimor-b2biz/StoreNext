## FINAL POST

128 people. One room. And for the first time, the team from Memed 7 sitting alongside us.

That says more than any slide deck could.

This week we held our annual company conference. The venue was the National Library of Israel in Jerusalem. Keeper of the printed treasures of the Jewish people across generations. A building that takes seriously the idea of preserving what matters.

We reviewed the past year. It was not an easy year for anyone in Israel. Complex. At times, heavy. And yet the results were exceptional. Bold decisions, tight teamwork, and focus on what counts. That combination worked. Again.

We recognized employees for longevity and excellence. People who have stayed, built, and carried the work forward.

And we ended with a tour of handwritten manuscripts, ancient documents, and records of Jewish life spanning centuries. In a year that reminded all of us why these things matter, that closing was earned.

A company is not its product roadmap. It is the people who show up, the culture they build, and how they handle a hard year.

We are proud of both.

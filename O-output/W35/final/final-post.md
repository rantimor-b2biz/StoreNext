Procurement digitization already returns 2.7x to 6.6x median ROI. Most organizations still fail to capture it.

The reason is not the technology. It is execution.

According to JAGGAER's 2026 benchmark, that 2.7x to 6.6x median holds across more than 200 global projects. Peak returns reach 58x. No AI required.

So why do so many organizations miss the range?

The same research points to a data problem. 74% of procurement leaders say their data is not AI-ready, according to The Hackett Group. Meanwhile 94% use generative AI weekly, but only 4% have scaled it.

The blocker is structural. Supplier data is fragmented by design.

Vendor documents arrive by email. Compliance certificates sit in unindexed PDFs. Pricing lives in one system, payment history in another. None of it reconciles automatically against the ERP.

AI cannot reason over data it cannot parse.

This is exactly where a structured supplier collaboration layer becomes critical. It turns scattered supplier interactions into validated, ERP-ready records.

- Validated supplier records, captured once in a consistent format
- A complete audit trail across every document and approval
- ERP-ready data that reconciles automatically
- A single source of truth per vendor

These are the same conditions that produce documented ROI now. They are also the exact data foundation AI will need later.

Digitizing supplier workflows pays twice. A measurable return today. The data infrastructure any future AI layer requires.

The ROI case is already proven. The open question is whether the supplier data foundation is being built to capture it.

If you are planning 2026 procurement budgets, which side of the execution gap is your organization on?

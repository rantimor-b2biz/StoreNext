Israel's Tax Authority planned this e-invoicing rollout for 2028.

Now it lands by June 2026.

The CTC allocation number mandate just compressed a multi-year timeline into six months. Per Sovos, VAT Implementation Order 01/2025 skips the planned NIS 15,000 step entirely.

Here is what actually changes.

From January 1, 2026, invoices above NIS 10,000 need a pre-approved allocation number. From June 1, 2026, the threshold drops to NIS 5,000.

Without that number, the input VAT deduction can be disqualified.

The problem is not the rule. It is the volume.

At NIS 5,000, qualifying invoices multiply. Every one needs a Tax Authority-reviewed number, matched to invoice data, before VAT can be claimed. Manual validation does not scale with that count. It scales worse.

This is exactly where a supplier portal becomes the compliance gate in front of the ERP:

- OCR reads the allocation number on every incoming invoice
- The number is validated against the invoice amount and the legal thresholds
- Non-compliant invoices stop in the portal. They never reach the ERP.
- Where permitted, the portal assigns and stamps the allocation number itself

Deloitte reports 50% of CFOs rank digital transformation as their top 2026 priority. This mandate does not create that priority. It moves the deadline forward.

The January threshold is a rehearsal. The June threshold is the real test.

How are you sizing invoice volume at the NIS 5,000 threshold before June?

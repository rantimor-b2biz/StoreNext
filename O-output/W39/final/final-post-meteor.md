Your bank fees are rising. Can you see the full picture?

According to AFP's 2026 Commercial Account Analysis Benchmarks, banks are pushing fees higher after several years of more moderate increases.

The research draws on 85,000+ account analysis statements across 100+ banks worldwide.

That scale reveals a familiar treasury challenge.

Multiple banks. Multiple entities. Different fee structures, statement formats and billing cycles.

The result?

Comparing banking costs across the enterprise becomes a manual, fragmented exercise.

And when fee information is scattered, treasury teams have less visibility into what they're paying, where costs are changing and which banking relationships deserve attention.

This is where connected treasury infrastructure matters.

A consolidated view across banking relationships creates the foundation for:

- Greater visibility into banking costs
- Better identification of changes and exceptions
- More informed bank relationship management
- Stronger, data-driven negotiations

Visibility is not just about knowing what you paid. It's about having the information to influence what you pay next.

Before your next banking renewal, ask yourself:

Can you see the full cost of your banking relationships in one place?

# Meteor exhibition print specification

Date: 2026-08-22

## Delivered artwork

- Rollup: 850 x 2000 mm trim.
- Counter: 1200 x 900 mm trim.
- Wall: 2000 x 2200 mm trim.
- Each PDF includes 5 mm bleed on all sides, crop marks, an explicit TrimBox, BleedBox and ArtBox, CMYK vector artwork, and embedded Arial fonts.
- The original supplied Meteor SVG logo is retained as vector paths.
- The QR area is intentionally empty and labelled "Scan to enter" for later placement.

## Production assumptions

- No vendor dieline, ICC profile, or hardware template was supplied. Before printing, the printer should confirm bleed, finishing, wrap, pole-pocket, seam, and hidden-bottom requirements.
- Rollup mechanisms often hide approximately 100-150 mm at the bottom. The current artwork keeps essential content above that area, but the printer's exact template takes precedence.
- Fabric walls and counters may require larger bleed, panel splitting, wraps, or mirrored edges. The current files are flat artwork at the requested finished dimensions.

## Watch asset note

The rollup uses the standalone transparent Apple Watch PNG supplied by the user. No substitute, crop from the composite, or redrawing is used. The source was carefully upscaled and converted to CMYK for print placement while preserving its proportions.

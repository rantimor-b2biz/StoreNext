## Key Intelligence

**The Problem:**
- Most procurement operations run on email, PDF invoices, and Excel trackers
- AI models require structured, consistent, machine-readable data
- Unstructured procurement data produces unreliable AI outputs — "garbage in, garbage out"
- McKinsey (2024): only 20% of procurement organizations have data infrastructure mature enough to support AI-driven automation

**The Insight:**
- A supplier portal creates structured, tagged, queryable data at transaction level
- Every supplier interaction, document, and approval becomes part of a clean data record
- This is not the AI layer. It is the prerequisite for it.

**StoreNext Proof Points:**
- 2 million transactions per day, 300+ Enterprise clients
- Every transaction creates structured, consistent data
- This infrastructure is what enables the automation and AI layer above it

**Industry Data:**
- Deloitte (2024): organizations with structured supplier data report 60% faster decision-making
- Gartner (2025): 74% of CPOs plan to deploy AI in procurement by 2027 — but fewer than 30% have the data infrastructure to support it
- The Hackett Group (2025): structured procurement data is the #1 prerequisite cited for successful AI implementation

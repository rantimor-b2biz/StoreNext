### Stat #2 — The Efficiency Gap (Hackett Group / Suplari, 2025-2026)

Procurement workloads will rise 10% while budgets increase only 1%, creating a 9% efficiency gap that must be closed through productivity gains. Some procurement teams are already capturing 15–30% efficiency improvements through AI automation. For 41% of CPOs, digital transformation is the strategy expected to deliver the most value.


**Label:** **Fact** — Hackett Group 2025 Survey, cited by Suplari/ProcurementTactics.
**StoreNext relevance:** Teams are being asked to do more with the same headcount. A supplier portal that reduces inbound calls, email traffic, and manual status checks is a direct answer to this gap.

## Track C — Israeli Context

**Key finding:** The Israeli CFO is under mounting pressure to digitize — and is increasingly open to technology.


Despite a recent slowdown in AI growth, the CFO is now a technology buyer who needs to make decisions fast. Today, CFOs are no longer just evaluating tools, but searching for a fix for fundamentally broken revenue systems. The traditional cautious CFO is becoming less common.



Israel dominates cybersecurity and represents nearly half of global spend because it understands the CISO. The Israeli ecosystem can recreate that success with CFOs who are overlooked, in need of urgent solutions, and more open to new technology than ever before.


**Israeli public procurement digitization:** 
Israel's procurement policies are constantly evolving, with digitization and e-procurement being a significant focus — more contracting authorities are moving towards online systems for notices, document release, and bid submission.


**Israeli government AI investment (January 2026):** 
The National Digital Directorate and Ministry of Innovation announced 13 new government projects to integrate AI into government ministries — a large-scale initiative to streamline work processes and move the public sector into the era of artificial intelligence, with total investment of approximately NIS 40 million.


**Signal:** The Israeli market is actively digitizing procurement at both the public and private sector level. StoreNext is positioned at exactly the right moment.

**Security/Economy Context:**

Israel's economy is beginning to feel the impact of the Iran war, particularly labor shortages among prime-age workers mobilized for conflict and weaker consumer spending due to safety concerns. Tourism has also been severely impacted, further weighing on growth and government revenues.


**CFO Pressure is Acute:**
The combination of war-related budget pressure and economic uncertainty creates a very specific CFO mindset in Israel right now: **do more with existing resources, prove ROI fast, reduce operational friction**. This is fertile ground for StoreNext's core messaging.

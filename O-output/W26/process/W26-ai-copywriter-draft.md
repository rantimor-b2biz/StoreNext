## Draft v1

Everyone is asking: when do we get AI in procurement?
The better question: what will the AI actually read?

Most procurement operations run on email threads, PDF invoices, and spreadsheet trackers. The data exists. It is not structured. It is not consistent. It is not machine-readable.

AI does not fix messy data. AI reflects it.

The organizations deploying effective procurement automation started with a different project. Not an AI project. A data infrastructure project.

Structured supplier communication. Consistent document formats. Approval records in a single system. Every transaction tagged and queryable.

That is what a supplier portal actually builds. Not just a better workflow. The data layer that makes AI viable.

At StoreNext, 2 million transactions per day flow through our platform across 300+ Enterprise clients. Every one creates structured, consistent data. That infrastructure is what enables the next layer.

The companies ready for procurement AI are not the ones with the largest AI budgets. They are the ones that built clean infrastructure first.

Is your procurement data ready to be read by a machine?

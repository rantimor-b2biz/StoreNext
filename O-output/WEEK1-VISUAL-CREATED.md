## 🚨 Troubleshooting

**"Colors don't look right"**
→ Ensure your monitor color profile is sRGB. Colors should be:
  - Purple background: Deep, professional, not bright
  - Turquoise: Bright, readable on purple
  - Red: Bold accent, not overwhelming
  - White: Crisp and readable

**"SVG won't display in browser"**
→ Use `week1-visual-html.html` instead. Open in Chrome/Firefox.

**"Text is blurry/pixelated"**
→ When converting SVG to PNG, ensure 1x scale (not 0.5x or 2x).

**"File too large"**
→ Compress PNG using: https://tinypng.com/ (maintains quality)

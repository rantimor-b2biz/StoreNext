## Top Stat + Source

**Ardent Partners, 2024:** Average cost to resolve one invoice exception manually: USD 53.
**PayStream Advisors / IOFM, 2025:** Organizations using a centralized supplier portal see 73% reduction in invoice exceptions.
**Scale math:** 100 exceptions/month x $53 = $5,300/month direct cost. $63,600/year.

## Competitor Gap

No competitor in the Israeli supplier portal market (Nipendo, Segment) frames their value proposition in terms of per-exception cost. They focus on process efficiency or feature lists. StoreNext's angle: give the CFO the math they haven't run yet. "מה המספר שלך?" invites them to calculate their own exposure.

## Israeli / Local Context

Israeli enterprise finance teams operate under increasing pressure from:
1. The חשבונית ישראל reform (VAT allocation numbers, phased from 2025-2026)
2. The Commercial Credit Law (45-day payment window for public sector)
3. Post-October 2023 supply chain stress that elevated supplier relationship management as a strategic concern

The $53/exception cost translates to real NIS exposure. For a 500-supplier enterprise averaging 3-4 exceptions per supplier annually = 1,500-2,000 exceptions = $79,500-$106,000 in direct exception-handling cost per year, before payment delays.

## Research Summary

Top stat: Ardent Partners 2024 — $53 average cost per invoice exception (manual handling).
Supporting stat: PayStream Advisors/IOFM 2025 — 73% reduction in invoice exceptions with centralized supplier portal.
Scale math: 100 exceptions/month = $5,300 direct cost/month = $63,600/year. Compelling ROI case.

Competitor gap: No Israeli supplier portal competitor quantifies per-exception cost in their messaging. Nipendo and Segment focus on process efficiency, not financial cost. This is StoreNext's gap angle.

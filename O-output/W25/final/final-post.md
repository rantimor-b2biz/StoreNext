Each invoice exception costs your organization $53 on average.

That figure covers only the direct handling cost.
(Ardent Partners, 2024)

It does not include follow-up calls to suppliers. It does not include payment delays or the internal hours spent resolving discrepancies.

The fully loaded cost is higher.

At 100 exceptions per month, the direct line item is $5,300. Every month. Every year.

Finance leaders often categorize this under operational inefficiency. That framing understates the problem.

Invoice exceptions are a cash flow issue. They delay payments, strain supplier relationships, and consume finance team capacity that should be directed elsewhere.

Organizations with a centralized supplier portal report 73% fewer invoice exceptions.

The mechanism is straightforward. Suppliers submit structured, validated data. Exceptions drop before they reach accounts payable.

Your finance team should be managing working capital and vendor relationships. Not manually resolving mismatches.

What does one invoice exception actually cost your organization?

## POST 2 — W25 (ESG/CSRD)

By 2027, Israeli companies exporting to Europe must submit ESG documentation on their supply chain.

CSRD does not ask about intentions. It asks: "Do you have the data?"

71% of senior Procurement leaders expect ESG requirements from suppliers to become law within 3 years. Only 28% can collect that data today. (Deloitte, 2025)

This is not a knowledge gap. It is an infrastructure gap.

Israel's Securities Authority published draft ESG disclosure requirements for public companies in 2025. 60+ listed companies will need to report on their supply chains within 24 months.

You do not need to build a new system.

A supplier portal with an ESG questionnaire in the Onboarding flow does the work. Every supplier who registers completes a disclosure. Every invoice is logged with compliance documentation. Every data point is audit-ready.

Your existing portal can already serve as your ESG infrastructure. What is missing is the configuration.

How many of your active suppliers could send you a verified ESG disclosure today? What is the number?

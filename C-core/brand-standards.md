# StoreNext Brand Standards

**Purpose:** Ensure consistent, professional brand representation across all marketing channels

---

## Brand Colors

### Primary Palette
- **Primary Blue:** #003D82 (Trust, professionalism, enterprise stability)
- **Secondary Gray:** #47525E (Professional, neutral, sophisticated)
- **Accent Teal:** #00A896 (Modern, innovative, forward-thinking)

### Usage
- **Primary Blue:** Dominant in logos, headers, buttons
- **Secondary Gray:** Backgrounds, text, supporting elements
- **Accent Teal:** CTAs, highlights, emphasis
- **White Space:** Essential for enterprise aesthetic

### Color Combinations (Acceptable)
- Navy Blue + White (professional, clean)
- Navy Blue + Teal (professional + modern)
- Gray + White (neutral, minimal)
- Navy + Gray + Teal (complete palette)

### Color Combinations (Avoid)
- Too many colors (max 3 + white)
- Bright, saturated colors
- Pastels or soft colors
- Red, orange, yellow (too casual)

---

## Typography

### Primary Font
- **Headings:** Sans-serif (professional, clean)
- **Body Text:** Sans-serif (modern, readable)
- **Examples:** Helvetica, Segoe UI, Roboto, Inter

### Font Sizes
- **Article Headlines:** 28-36px
- **Section Headers:** 20-24px
- **Body Text:** 14-16px (mobile: 14px minimum)
- **Captions:** 12-13px

### Font Weights
- **Headlines:** Bold (700+)
- **Subheadings:** Semi-bold (600)
- **Body Text:** Regular (400)
- **Emphasis:** Semi-bold (600)

### Line Length
- **Web:** 50-75 characters per line
- **Mobile:** 30-50 characters per line
- **Articles:** Break paragraphs into 3-4 sentence chunks
- **LinkedIn:** Very short lines (15-20 words max)

## Visual Style

### Imagery Preferences
- **Professional photography** (actual business scenarios)
- **Real people in work contexts** (not stock photo smiles)
- **Data visualizations** (charts, graphs, metrics)
- **Organizational diagrams** (structure, clarity)
- **Supply chain flows** (process visualization)

### Photography Guidelines
- Professional lighting, high quality
- Business-appropriate dress and settings
- Diverse representation
- Real work environments (not staged)
- Avoid: Overly staged, too casual, emotionally manipulative

### Graphic Styles
- **Clean, minimalist** (not cluttered)
- **Data-forward** (charts, numbers, metrics)
- **Structured layouts** (not organic, flowing designs)
- **Professional color palette** (navy, gray, teal)
- **Typography-focused** (text as design element)

---

## Visual Metaphors

### What to Use
- **Organizational structures** (flow charts, hierarchies)
- **Data visualization** (charts, graphs, trending)
- **Supply chain networks** (connections, nodes, flow)
- **Professional handshakes** (partnership, collaboration)
- **Buildings/architecture** (structure, scale, enterprise)
- **Precision imagery** (measurement, accuracy, quality)

### What to Avoid
- **Abstract art** (too subjective, not enterprise-appropriate)
- **Overly emotional imagery** (stock photo sadness/joy)
- **Nature metaphors** (too personal, not business-focused)
- **Bright, playful colors** (not professional)
- **People looking thoughtful with chins on hands** (cliché)

---

## Tone & Language

### Professional Standards
- **Formal:** 8/10 (Professional, authoritative, not stiff)
- **Straightforward:** No marketing fluff or jargon without context
- **Data-driven:** Claims backed by numbers and metrics
- **Solution-focused:** Problem → Solution → Result

### What to Use
- Active voice ("You reduce costs..." not "Costs can be reduced...")
- Short sentences (10-14 words)
- Business terminology (procurement, vendor, supply chain, etc.)
- Concrete examples with real numbers
- Clear calls-to-action

### What to Avoid
- Marketing hyperbole (Revolutionary, Transformative, Disruptive)
- Casual language (Let's connect, the journey, synergy)
- Jargon without explanation
- Exclamation marks (use periods)
- Em dashes (use periods or rewrite)
- Questions as hooks (use statements)

## Logo & Brand Mark

### Logo Specifications
- **Minimum size:** 100px wide
- **Clear space:** Minimum 10% around logo
- **Color versions:** Full color (navy), white (on dark), grayscale
- **Never:** Stretch, rotate, change colors (except official variations)

### Logo Usage
- On all external communications
- Consistent placement and sizing
- Minimum white space around
- Professional, clean contexts only

---

## Design Elements

### Icon Style
- Clean, minimalist
- Professional and business-appropriate
- Consistent stroke weight
- Monochromatic (single color or white)
- 24px grid alignment

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Style:** Solid color (primary blue or teal)
- **Text:** Clear, action-oriented ("Get Demo", "Request Proposal", "Learn More")
- **Accessibility:** Sufficient contrast ratio (WCAG AA compliant)

### Spacing & Layout
- **Margins:** Minimum 15px (mobile), 20px (desktop)
- **Padding:** Generous white space
- **Grid:** 12-column layout (responsive)
- **Alignment:** Left-aligned text (right-align numbers/metrics)

---

## Content Guidelines

### Article Format
- **Headline:** 50-70 characters, benefit-driven
- **Subheading:** Optional, clarifies headline
- **Body:** Short paragraphs (3-4 sentences)
- **Headers:** Use H2 and H3 (not H1 except headline)
- **Lists:** Bulleted, not numbered (unless sequence matters)

### LinkedIn Post Format
- **Length:** 150-300 words
- **Line breaks:** Every 2-3 sentences
- **Emoji:** Minimal (1-2 max, professional only)
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant hashtags
- **CTA:** Clear next step (comment, message, click)

### Email Format
- **Subject line:** 45-50 characters, benefit-driven
- **Preview text:** 90-100 characters, extends subject line
- **Body:** Very short paragraphs, mobile-first
- **CTA:** Primary (1-2), secondary (optional)
- **Signature:** Professional title, company, contact info

---

## Accessibility Standards

### Color Contrast
- Text on background: Minimum 4.5:1 (WCAG AA)
- All images have alt text
- Links underlined or clearly distinguishable
- No information conveyed by color alone

### Mobile Design
- Minimum font size: 14px
- Touch targets: Minimum 44x44px
- Responsive layouts for 320px - 2560px
- Short, scannable paragraphs

### Video Content
- Closed captions required
- Audio descriptions for important visual elements
- Transcript provided

---

## Prohibited Elements

❌ **Never Use:**
- Bright, neon colors
- Comic Sans or novelty fonts
- Stock photos of people in unnatural poses
- Em dashes in digital content
- Multiple font families (stick to 1-2)
- Centered body text (left-align)
- Light text on light backgrounds
- Autoplay videos with sound
- Blinking or flashing elements
- Overly large images (optimize for web)

## Visual Examples

### Good Design (Follows Standards)
```
Clean layout with navy headers, gray body text
Professional photography of business environment
Data visualization with teal accent highlights
Short paragraphs with generous white space
"Learn More" button in teal
```

```
Rainbow color scheme with bright reds and yellows
Stock photos of people making weird poses
Dense paragraph blocks with no white space
"Click here now!" button in orange
Comic Sans headlines
```

- **Authoritative** (credible, expert)

- We understand enterprise complexity
- We provide transparent partnerships

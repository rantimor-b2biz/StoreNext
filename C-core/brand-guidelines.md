### Typography

#### Font Family
**Primary Font:** Inter (for digital), Segoe UI (fallback)
- Clean, modern, sans-serif
- Excellent readability at all sizes
- Professional and technical feel

#### Type Hierarchy

| Level | Size (Desktop) | Weight | Usage |
|-------|---|--------|-------|
| **H1 - Page Title** | 48–54pt | Bold (700) | Main page headlines |
| **H2 - Section Header** | 36–42pt | Bold (700) | Major sections |
| **H3 - Subsection** | 28–32pt | Semi-bold (600) | Subsections, feature groups |
| **H4 - Heading** | 20–24pt | Semi-bold (600) | Component headers |
| **Body Text** | 16–18pt | Regular (400) | Main content, descriptions |
| **Small Text** | 14pt | Regular (400) | Captions, metadata |
| **Micro Text** | 12pt | Regular (400) | Footnotes, legal, timestamps |

#### Typography Rules

**Headers:**
- Always bold or semi-bold
- Sentence case (capitalize first word + proper nouns only)
- Maximum 50 characters for optimal readability
- Line-height: 1.2

**Body Text:**
- Regular weight, never light
- 16–18pt for accessibility
- Line-height: 1.5
- Line length: 60–80 characters max (readability)
- Max 4 sentences per paragraph

**Emphasis:**
- Use **bold** for key terms, not italics
- Use color for highlighting data, not size
- Lists instead of long paragraphs

## 🎨 Visual Components

### Imagery & Photography

**What We Use:**
- ✅ Real procurement and finance teams at work
- ✅ Screenshots of actual platform interfaces
- ✅ Data visualizations (charts, graphs, dashboards)
- ✅ Professional photography of diverse teams
- ✅ Infographics explaining complex processes

**What We Avoid:**
- ❌ Stock photos of people in unrealistic scenarios ("happy people high-fiving")
- ❌ Generic tech imagery (abstract servers, code on screens)
- ❌ Cartoons or overly stylized illustrations
- ❌ Clichéd business imagery (handshakes, boardrooms)
- ❌ Unrelated or random visuals

**Standards:**
- High resolution (minimum 300 DPI for print, 72 DPI for web)
- Consistent color grading
- People are diverse and realistic
- Always attribute or source correctly

### Icons & Graphics

**Icon Style:**
- Line-based, clean, 24–48px
- Consistent stroke weight (2px minimum)
- Rounded corners (2–4px)
- Color-coded where possible (purple headers, turquoise accents)

**Data Visualizations:**
- Use purple for primary data series
- Use turquoise for secondary/comparative
- Use red for alerts or deviations
- Include clear labels and legends
- Avoid 3D effects or unnecessary complexity

### Design Spacing

**Consistent Grid System:**
- 8px baseline grid
- 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px common gaps
- 16px minimum padding around content
- 24px margins between major sections

## 📝 Logo Usage

**StoreNext Logo (Horizontal)**
- Minimum size: 200px width
- Clear space: 50% of logo height
- Always use full color on white/light backgrounds
- Grayscale version available for limited-color contexts
- Never modify proportions, rotate, or add effects

**Logo Placement:**
- Bottom right corner (standard web placement)
- Top left (website header, document header)
- Never overlay logo on image content without contrast adjustment

## 💬 Written Communication

### Messaging Hierarchy

**Tagline (13 characters):**
"Enterprise Infrastructure"

**Mission Statement (1 line):**
"We enable Enterprise organizations to manage procurement, finance, and data with visibility, control, and efficiency at scale."

**Brand Promise (1 sentence):**
"StoreNext is the infrastructure Enterprise teams trust to transform procurement from email-chaos to strategic advantage."

### Voice Attributes (Detailed)

**Technical + Accessible**
- We know Enterprise systems (ERP, P2P, DSO, compliance)
- We explain them plainly
- Example: "Direct SAP integration" not "blockchain-powered cloud"

**Confident + Transparent**
- We lead with proof: 300+ clients, 3M transactions/day, 20B+ ₪ processed
- We're honest about what takes effort
- Example: "Implementation takes weeks, not months" not "Easy setup"

**Data-Driven + Human**
- Every claim backed by numbers
- Professional but warm tone
- Example: "15–20% reduction in AP processing time" not "Amazing results"

**Enterprise-Focused + Approachable**
- We respect the seriousness of their operations
- We're still human and clear
- Example: "Compliance-ready, not compliance-obsessed" not "Boring enterprise tool"

### Communication Style Guide

**DO:**
- ✅ Use specific metrics: "80% reduction in supplier calls" not "better efficiency"
- ✅ Lead with the problem: "Your team answers 500+ supplier emails per month"
- ✅ Back up claims: Always include data or proof
- ✅ Be direct: Short sentences, active voice
- ✅ Show respect: Acknowledge complexity without dismissing it
- ✅ Make data visual: Use charts, graphs, numbers prominently

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Use hype: "Revolutionary," "transforming," "game-changing"
- ❌ Say "easy": Enterprise challenges aren't simple
- ❌ Be vague: "Thousands of customers" not "300+ Enterprise clients"
- ❌ Oversell: Let results speak, don't promise miracles
- ❌ Use buzzwords: AI, blockchain, disruptive, cutting-edge
- ❌ Sound corporate: Stay human and clear

### Key Messages by Persona

**For CFOs:**
"Real-time visibility into procurement spending. Visibility reduces DSO, improves cash flow, and eliminates manual work."

**For Procurement Leaders:**
"One supplier portal, one standard process, zero email. Supplier self-service means your team focuses on strategy, not status updates."

**For CIOs:**
"Direct ERP integration, 99.9% uptime SLA, SOC 2 certified. Infrastructure that enhances your systems, doesn't disrupt them."

## 📱 Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Website

**Visual Standards:**
- White or light grey backgrounds
- Purple headers, turquoise accents
- Clean typography: Inter Bold for headers, Inter Regular for body
- 16px minimum font size
- Mobile-first responsive design
- High-quality case study and process imagery
- Data visualizations for complex concepts

**Tone:** Authoritative, clear, data-focused

**Example Page Flow:**
1. Hero: Problem statement + proof (no solution yet)
2. Product section: How StoreNext addresses it
3. Case studies: Real results from real customers
4. Social proof: Logos and testimonials
5. CTA: Schedule demo or download resource

### LinkedIn

**Visual Standards:**
- Graphic posts: Purple background + turquoise/red accents
- Text-heavy format encouraged (LinkedIn algorithm favors longer posts)
- Include specific metrics/proof points
- Real customer data when possible

**Tone:** Conversational, authoritative, specific

**Post Types:**
- Problem awareness (leading with pain, not solution)
- Thought leadership (future of procurement)
- Social proof (customer quotes with results)
- Educational content (KPIs, best practices)
- Direct CTA (schedule demo, webinar, download)

**Engagement Strategy:**
- Monday 5:00 PM publish time
- Respond to ALL comments within 30 minutes
- Ask specific questions in comments
- Share team members' professional networks

### Email

**Visual Standards:**
- Clean, minimal design
- Purple CTA buttons with turquoise hover
- 600px width (email-safe)
- White background, dark text
- 50% image to 50% text ratio maximum

**Tone:** Professional but warm, personal, direct

**Structure:**
1. Subject line: Specific benefit or question (avoid hype)
2. Preview text: Hook or context
3. Greeting: By name if possible
4. Opening: Relevant to recipient's role
5. Body: Value first, then product mention
6. CTA: Single, clear, specific button
7. Signature: Name, title, contact info

### Print / PDF

**Standards:**
- Full color when possible
- Maintain 1" margins minimum
- Purple headers, turquoise accents
- High-resolution imagery (300 DPI)
- Consistent typography hierarchy
- Page numbers and dates on documents
- Company logo on header or footer

## ✅ Quality Standards & Checklists

### Brand Alignment Checklist

Before publishing any content:

- [ ] Positions StoreNext as **infrastructure**, not a tool
- [ ] Addresses **Enterprise complexity appropriately** (doesn't oversimplify)
- [ ] Speaks to **correct personas** (CFO, Procurement Leader, or CIO — or signal between them)
- [ ] Uses **data-driven proof** (specific metrics, not hype)
- [ ] Professional tone: **technical, confident, transparent**
- [ ] No buzzwords or vague language
- [ ] Consistent with established messaging
- [ ] All claims supported by proof or data

### Visual Quality Checklist

- [ ] Colors match brand palette (purple #3C2C4C, turquoise #7CCBC3)
- [ ] Typography uses Inter Bold (headers) and Inter Regular (body)
- [ ] Minimum font size 16px (body), 24px (headers)
- [ ] High-resolution imagery (minimum 1200px width for web)
- [ ] Adequate white space (16px minimum padding)
- [ ] Logo placed correctly (bottom right or top left)
- [ ] Color contrast accessible (WCAG AA standard minimum)
- [ ] Mobile responsive tested
- [ ] No stock photo clichés (realistic imagery only)

## 📞 Questions & Governance

**Who approves brand changes?**
- **Minor adjustments:** Marketing Director (no brand impact)
- **Color/typography changes:** Leadership approval required
- **Major brand shifts:** Founder/CEO approval required

**How do we handle exceptions?**
- Exceptions documented with approval signature
- Tracked in M-memory/brand-exceptions.md
- Reviewed quarterly for incorporation into guidelines

**Visual feedback process:**
1. Design created per guidelines
2. Review checklist completed
3. Brand Alignment Checklist checked (all items pass)
4. Approval from Marketing Director
5. Publication or distribution

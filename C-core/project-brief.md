# Project Brief

> StoreNext Group — Enterprise Procurement, Finance Automation & Data Intelligence Infrastructure

---

## About StoreNext Group

**What we do:** StoreNext Group provides a comprehensive digital infrastructure for managing procurement, supply chain finance, and business intelligence for Enterprise organizations in Israel.

**Who we serve:** Enterprise organizations (300–2,000+ employees) with complex procurement operations, multiple suppliers, multiple bank accounts, and significant daily transaction volumes.

**Our mission:** To help Enterprise organizations gain complete control, transparency, and intelligence over their supply chain, financial flows, and business data — enabling them to operate at scale with confidence.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Enterprise organizations struggle with fragmented, manual procurement and financial processes that don't scale. When you have:

- Hundreds or thousands of suppliers
- Multiple bank accounts and payment channels
- Daily transaction volumes in the thousands
- Complex ERP systems
- Regulatory requirements

**Before StoreNext:**
- Procurement is email-based and uncontrolled
- No visibility into payment status
- Finance team is overwhelmed with manual work
- No unified view of supplier commitments
- Risk of payment delays and supplier disputes
- Limited ability to forecast cash flow
- Data is scattered across systems

**After StoreNext:**
- Automated supplier portal and communication
- Real-time visibility into all transactions
- Finance team focused on strategy, not admin
- Single source of truth for procurement data
- Complete audit trail and compliance
- Data-driven forecasting and insights
- Enterprise scale with confidence

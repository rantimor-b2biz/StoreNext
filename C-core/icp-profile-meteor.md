## Overview

Meteor is a financial management infrastructure for enterprise organizations with complex financial operations.

It centralizes bank data, treasury management, payments, and compliance into a unified system integrated with ERP platforms.

Meteor replaces fragmented workflows and Excel-based processes with a single system for enterprise finance teams — across banks, payments, treasury, and compliance.

---

## Target Entity

Israeli enterprise organizations with:

- Revenues of 12M+ NIS annually
- Complex financial operations
- Multiple bank accounts (5+)
- Multiple banking relationships
- ERP systems in place (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Priority, Dynamics)
- High financial transaction volume (hundreds to thousands daily)
- Multi-entity or multi-subsidiary structures
- Finance teams (5+ employees)
- Exposure to regulatory frameworks (IFRS16, audits)

## Buyer Personas

### CFO
- **Needs:** Control, visibility, compliance
- **Pain:** Fragmented systems, Excel dependency, risk exposure
- **Decision role:** Primary budget owner and final approver

### Controller
- **Needs:** Accuracy, audit readiness
- **Pain:** Manual work, errors, lack of traceability
- **Decision role:** Key influencer, day-to-day user

### Treasurer
- **Needs:** Liquidity control, forecasting
- **Pain:** Uncertainty, lack of centralized data
- **Decision role:** Primary operational user

### CIO / IT
- **Needs:** Integration, stability, scalability
- **Pain:** Disconnected systems, maintenance overhead
- **Decision role:** Technical gatekeeper

---

## Buying Process

- **Sales cycle:** 1–4 months
- **Decision drivers:** Acute operational pain (not long-term strategic planning)
- **Entry point:** Usually CFO or Controller, IT involved for integration sign-off
- **Process:** Demo → Proof of concept → Procurement approval
- **Note:** Shorter cycle than typical enterprise — decision is often triggered by a specific event (audit, ERP migration, team growth)

---

## Triggers

- ERP implementation or migration
- Regulatory audit or compliance review
- IPO preparation
- Growth in financial complexity
- Expansion to multiple entities or regions
- Increase in number of bank accounts
- Finance transformation initiatives

---

## Disqualifiers

- Single bank account
- Low transaction volume
- No ERP system
- Small finance team
- Simple financial structure
- Revenues below 12M NIS

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors (Israel):** סילברנט, NILUS, Finisite

**Positioning vs. competitors:**
- Meteor competes on depth of ERP integrations, breadth of modules (treasury + payments + IFRS16 in one system), and AI-native capabilities

## Key Insight

Meteor becomes relevant when financial complexity exceeds the ability to manage operations through spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

The trigger is usually a specific event — an audit, a new ERP, a merger, a new CFO — not a slow strategic decision.

Procurement Platform – SRM, Pre-Procurement, Order Management, SCM & Invoice Payments

## SRM (Supplier Relationship Management)

### Create a single, governed foundation for supplier relationships and supplier data.

Standardise onboarding, maintain clean supplier master data, and keep collaboration structured so issues are prevented before they reach POs, deliveries, or invoices.


Functional Coverage:

- Supplier Registration
- Supplier Master Data Management
- Supplier Enablement


[Explore Area](https://nipendo.com/srm/)

## PRE-PROCUREMENT

### Align commercial decisions with operational execution before anything becomes a PO.

Manage sourcing and requests, consolidate inputs from suppliers, and keep agreements, catalogues, and price lists enforceable so fewer exceptions appear downstream.


Functional Coverage:

- Sourcing and RFI
- RFx and Proposals
- Bids and Auctions
- Agreement Management
- Catalogue and Pricing
- Requisitions and Forecasts

[Explore Area](https://nipendo.com/pre-procurement_new/)

## ORDER MGMT

### Streamline your purchasing process from order to delivery.

Create, approve, and track purchase orders with full visibility across the procurement lifecycle.


Functional Coverage:

- Purchase Order Creation
- Order Approval Workflow
- Order Tracking
- Delivery Management

[Explore Area](https://nipendo.com/order-management_new/)

## SCM (Supply Chain Management)

### Connect execution data to the process so deliveries are trackable, accountable, and ready for financial closure.

Coordinate milestones, approvals, shipping and freight activities, and ensure goods receipt and quality steps are captured consistently.


Functional Coverage:

- Milestone Reports
- Service Approval
- ASN and Serial Number
- Goods Receipt
- Quality Control
- Import and Freight
- Courier Integration
- Shipping

[Explore Area](https://nipendo.com/scm_new/)

## INVOICE AND PAYMENTS

### Automate invoice processing and payment execution while strengthening controls and traceability.

Handle multiple invoice types and payment scenarios, reduce mismatches, and route only true exceptions for review.


Functional Coverage:

- e-Invoicing
- Proforma
- Debit and Credit
- AP Automation
- ERS
- Remittance Advice
- Payment Status
- Cross Border Payments
- Dynamic Discounting
- AR Financing

## Calendar Building Process

### Step 1: Check Procurement Buyer Calendar

CFO and Procurement priorities shift by quarter:
- **Q1 (Jan-Mar):** Budget planning, annual vendor reviews, cost reduction targets
- **Q2 (Apr-Jun):** Mid-year performance check, supplier audits
- **Q3 (Jul-Sep):** Procurement modernization projects, tech evaluations
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec):** Year-end spend, budget defense, planning for next year

Match content to what CFOs are thinking about RIGHT NOW.

### Step 2: Select Topics

From StoreNext's core content pillars:
- **Supplier visibility** — The gap between what procurement knows and what is actually happening
- **Vendor consolidation** — ROI of reducing supplier complexity
- **Supply chain resilience** — Risk management before the next disruption
- **Procurement modernization** — Moving from manual to digital, ERP gaps
- **Cost optimization** — Real numbers, real benchmarks
- **Geopolitical context** — When market events create procurement urgency

**Mix rule:** At least 1 data-backed post per week. At least 1 CFO perspective piece per 2 weeks.

### Step 3: Assign Hook Formula

Every LinkedIn post uses one of the approved hooks (from `T-tools/skills/social-post-skill.md`):
- **Visibility Gap Hook** — "Your suppliers know X. Your organization finds out Y days later."
- **Operational Reality Hook** — "When disruption hits, procurement teams spend the first [time] just trying to understand what is happening."
- **Question Nobody Asks** — "Why does enterprise procurement still run on spreadsheets and emails?"
- **Stat + So What** — "[Stat]. For procurement teams without [capability], that means..."

## Rules

- **Every post needs a statistic or data point** — Enterprise audience requires credibility
- **CFO or Procurement perspective, not vendor perspective** — Write from their seat, not ours
- **No hype or urgency language** — "Every enterprise" and "you must" are red flags
- **No em dashes (—)** — Hard rule. Use periods, colons, or commas instead.
- **Check gold standard post (M-memory)** — New posts should follow that format and level of quality

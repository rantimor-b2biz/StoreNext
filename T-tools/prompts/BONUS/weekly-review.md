# Weekly Review — StoreNext

Close the loop. What worked this week, what didn't, what to do differently.

## The 5 Questions

### 1. What did we publish this week?
List: LinkedIn posts, blog posts, newsletter sends.

### 2. What performed best?
LinkedIn: impressions, comments, profile visits, DMs
Blog: views, time on page, CTAs clicked
Newsletter: open rate, click rate

What SPECIFICALLY made the winner work? (Topic? Hook type? Format? Timing?)

### 3. What underperformed?
Honest diagnosis: Hook? Topic? Too long? Wrong persona angle?

### 4. What pattern are we seeing? (After 3+ weeks)
- Which topics resonate with CFOs vs Procurement Leaders?
- Which hook types (visibility gap vs stat vs question) get most engagement?
- What does the StoreNext audience actually engage with vs what we assume?

### 5. One specific change next week?
Not "post more." One concrete adjustment.

---

## The Loop

**If a pattern appeared 3+ times:**
→ Add to `C-core/voice-dna.md` under "What Works"

**If gold standard content produced:**
→ Save to `M-memory/` under approved patterns (see existing gold standard post)

**Always:**
→ Update `M-memory/learning-log.md` with week's key learning

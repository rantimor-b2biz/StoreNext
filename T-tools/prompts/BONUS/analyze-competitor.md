# Analyze Competitor — StoreNext

Map how Coupa, SAP Ariba, Jaggaer, or Ivalua positions themselves. Find the gap StoreNext owns.

## Primary Competitors to Track

- **Coupa** — coupa.com — Enterprise spend management
- **SAP Ariba** — sap.com — Procurement module within SAP ecosystem
- **Jaggaer** — jaggaer.com — Source-to-pay platform
- **Ivalua** — ivalua.com — Procurement platform
- **Proactis** — proactis.com — Mid-market procurement

---

## Research Steps

1. Fetch competitor homepage + product pages
2. Check their LinkedIn company page: what content do they post?
3. Search G2 / Gartner Peer Insights for recent reviews
4. Search: `[competitor] vs [competitor] enterprise supplier portal reviews`
5. Check: recent news, product launches, new partnerships

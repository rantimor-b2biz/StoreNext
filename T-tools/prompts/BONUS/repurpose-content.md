# Repurpose Content — StoreNext

Turn one strong piece into 5 enterprise-ready formats.

## Repurposing Map

| From | Into | Effort |
|------|------|--------|
| LinkedIn post | Blog section / Newsletter | Low |
| Blog post (1,500 words) | 3-4 LinkedIn posts + newsletter | Medium |
| Case study | LinkedIn mini case + blog mention + email | Medium |
| Research brief | LinkedIn thought leadership post | Low |
| Event/crisis angle | LinkedIn post + blog follow-up | Low |

---

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Identify the Core Asset
What is the one insight, stat, or story that makes this piece valuable?

Write it in one sentence: "The core asset is: ___"

### Step 2: Extract the Pieces
- The hook / opening problem
- The key McKinsey/Gartner stat
- The operational pain point (specific questions CFO/Procurement asks)
- The product connection (how Supplier Portal addresses it)
- The insight close ("resilience is built before the crisis")

### Step 3: Rebuild for Each Format

**LinkedIn Post (from blog):**
- Take the hook + one key stat + one operational question
- Add a discussion question at end
- Max 250 words, bullets for readability

**Newsletter Mention (from LinkedIn):**
- Expand context: why this matters strategically, not just operationally
- Add a "what to do" recommendation
- 100-150 words

**LinkedIn Mini Case (from full case study):**
- Company type + challenge + 3 numbers + quote
- Ends with: "This is what supplier portal infrastructure looks like."

**Blog Section (from research brief):**
- Expand the analysis section
- Add the "how leading organizations address this" framework
- 300-400 words as a standalone section

---

## Quality Checks

- Does each repurposed piece work standalone?
- Is the enterprise voice intact (no hype, specific numbers)?
- Is the product mention natural (not sales-first)?
- No em dashes?

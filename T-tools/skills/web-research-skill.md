## Research Workflows

### Workflow 1: LinkedIn Content Research (Most Common)

**Goal:** Find data for a specific enterprise pain point angle.

**Steps:**
1. Search: `[topic] enterprise statistics 2026` (procurement, supplier, ERP)
2. Search: `[topic] CFO challenges 2026` OR `procurement leader pain points`
3. Search: `McKinsey Gartner [topic]` (credibility sources)
4. Fetch 2-3 top reports or articles
5. Check Supply Chain Dive for recent news angle (timeliness)

**Output:**
```markdown
## Content Research Brief: [Topic]

### Key Numbers (for LinkedIn credibility)
- [Stat + Gartner/McKinsey/source + date]
- [Stat + source + date]

### CFO/Procurement Pain Point
[How this data shows the real business problem]

### Current Events Angle (if any)
[Is there a news hook? Geopolitical? Market event?]

### Best LinkedIn Angles
- [Angle 1: problem awareness]
- [Angle 2: thought leadership]
- [Angle 3: social proof]
```

---

### Workflow 2: Competitor Analysis

**Goal:** Understand how Coupa / SAP Ariba / Jaggaer position themselves vs StoreNext.

**Steps:**
1. Fetch competitor homepage + product pages
2. Search: `[competitor name] reviews G2 2026`
3. Search: `[competitor] vs [competitor] enterprise supplier portal`
4. Search: `[competitor] LinkedIn company page` — what content are they posting?

**Output:**
```markdown
## Competitor Brief: [Competitor]

**Category:** [What they are]
**Target:** [Who they explicitly target — size, industry]
**Positioning claim:** [Their exact words]
**Pricing model:** [If available]
**Strengths:** [What they do well]
**Weaknesses/Gaps:** [Missing features, complaints in reviews]
**Content angle they own:** [What they post about on LinkedIn]

### StoreNext Differentiation
[How StoreNext is different — specifically]

### Content Gap to Exploit
[What angle or proof point Coupa/Ariba isn't owning]
```

---

### Workflow 3: Geopolitical/Market Event Research

**Goal:** Connect a real-world event to supply chain/procurement impact for timely LinkedIn content.

**Steps:**
1. Search: `[event] supply chain impact 2026`
2. Search: `[event] procurement disruption enterprise`
3. Fetch 2-3 credible sources (Reuters, FT, Supply Chain Dive)
4. Find the McKinsey/Gartner stat on supply chain disruption frequency

**Key stat always on hand:** "McKinsey: Companies expect supply chain disruptions lasting a month or more every 3.7 years."

## Research Quality Rules

- **Verify before trusting:** One source = claim. Three sources = fact.
- **Date everything:** Enterprise research dates matter. Under 12 months = solid.
- **Label clearly:**
  - **Fact:** Verified data with source (Gartner 2026 report)
  - **Signal:** Pattern observed ("5 LinkedIn posts from procurement leaders this month show...")
  - **Opinion:** Your interpretation (label it clearly)
- **CFO standard:** If a CFO would question the stat, verify it twice.

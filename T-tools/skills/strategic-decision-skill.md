# Strategic Decision Skill — StoreNext

Frame any strategic choice as a clear brief with recommendation.

## Decision Brief Structure

### 1. Decision Required (1 sentence)
Not "should we explore" — "Do we launch [X campaign] by Q2 or wait?"

### 2. Context (2-3 sentences)
Why now. What's the cost of not deciding.

### 3. Options (2-4)
For each: What it is / Pros / Cons / Estimated impact / Confidence

Always include "Do Nothing" with explicit cost.

### 4. Risk Assessment
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |

### 5. Recommendation
Go with [Option]. Confidence: [H/M/L].
Key condition: [What must be true for this to work]

### 6. Unknowns
What we don't know + how to resolve it.

### 7. Next Steps
Action — Owner — Date

## Decision Templates for StoreNext

### ICP Focus Decision
```
Decision: Double down on CFO (finance angle) or expand focus to Procurement Leaders?
Key factors: Current win rate by persona, deal size, sales cycle, content engagement by role
Common trap: Targeting both equally = owning neither
Default: CFO-led deals tend to have higher ACVs; Procurement-led have faster cycles
```

### Content Strategy Decision
```
Decision: Increase posting frequency from [X]/week to [Y]/week OR improve quality of existing cadence?
Key factors: Current engagement trends, Copywriter capacity, quality signal from recent posts
Common trap: More output ≠ more pipeline. Quality compounds; quantity fatigues.
```

### Campaign Investment Decision
```
Decision: Invest [budget] in LinkedIn Ads now OR wait until organic baseline is stronger?
Key factors: Current organic engagement rate, ICP targeting availability, competitor ad spend
Common trap: Running ads to cold audiences before organic proof points exist
```

### Market Segment Decision
```
Decision: Target [industry X] or [industry Y] with focused campaign?
Key factors: Supplier complexity in each industry, ERP concentration, competitive density
Israeli market angle: Which segment has the most active procurement transformation?
```

---

## Writing Rules

- **Frame before analyzing** — one clear sentence stating the decision
- **Numbers over adjectives** — "3 months faster, 2x the cost" not "significantly faster"
- **Make trade-offs explicit** — not "Option A is better" but "A wins on [X], loses on [Y]"
- **Recommend — don't hedge** — "Go with A. Confidence: Medium. Watch for [risk]."

---

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Decision = one clear sentence?
- [ ] All options have numbers (even estimates)?
- [ ] "Do Nothing" included?
- [ ] Recommendation takes a stand?
- [ ] Next steps: who, what, when?
- [ ] Scannable in 2 minutes?

---

*Clarity is the goal. A 2-minute read that leads to a decision beats a 20-minute report that leads to more meetings.*

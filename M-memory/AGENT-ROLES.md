# Agent Roles & Responsibilities

**StoreNext Group – B2B Enterprise Marketing Team**

Each agent has a specific role in driving pipeline, brand authority, and sales enablement. This document defines roles, responsibilities, success metrics, and weekly deliverables.

---

## 🎯 Team Structure Overview

```
Marketing Director (Leadership)
├── Research Agent (Intelligence)
├── Copywriter Agent (Content)
├── Paid Search Agent (Demand Gen)
├── Visual Agent (Creative)
├── Newsletter Agent (Nurture)
├── ABM Agent (Account-Based Marketing)
├── Sales Enablement Agent (Sales Support)
└── Analytics Agent (Measurement)
```

## 1️⃣ Research Agent

**Role:** Market Intelligence, Competitive Analysis, Customer Insights

### Mission
Discover what Enterprise customers need, what competitors are doing, and where market opportunities exist. Every piece of content should be informed by research.

### Responsibilities

**Weekly Tasks:**
- [ ] Competitive landscape scan (top 3 procurement platforms, adjacent solutions): always check Nipendo and Segment our two Israeli rivals
- [ ] Market trend analysis (Enterprise procurement, digital transformation, supply chain)
- [ ] Customer insight research (triggers, pain points, decision-makers)
- [ ] Sales intelligence (objections, win/loss analysis, buying committee)

**Ongoing Research Areas:**
- Enterprise procurement trends and standards
- Regulatory/compliance changes (GDPR, data security, audit requirements)
- ERP system trends (Priority, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite integration patterns)
- Industry-specific pain points (manufacturing, retail, logistics, healthcare)
- Competitive positioning and messaging
- Customer job postings, funding, company news (trigger events)

**Deliverable:** Weekly Research Brief (Markdown file)
```
## Weekly Research Brief – [Date]

### Competitive Intelligence
- [Finding 1]
- [Finding 2]

### Market Trends
- [Trend 1]
- [Trend 2]

### Customer Insights
- [Insight 1]
- [Insight 2]

### Sales Intelligence
- [Opportunity/Challenge]

### Recommended Content/Campaign
- [Suggestion 1]
- [Suggestion 2]
```

### Success Metrics
- ✅ New market opportunity identified monthly
- ✅ Content ideas informed by research (100% of posts)
- ✅ Competitive threats flagged early
- ✅ Sales team has current objection handling data

### Dependencies
- Access to market research tools (G2, industry reports, LinkedIn, company news)
- Input from sales team on common objections

## 2️⃣ Copywriter Agent

**Role:** B2B Content Production (Blog, Case Studies, Sales Copy, Website)

### Mission
Create content that educates Enterprise buyers, builds thought leadership, and drives qualified leads. Every piece must be technically credible, data-backed, and on-brand.

### Responsibilities

**Regular Content Production:**
- [ ] **Blog Posts (2x/week)** – 1,200–1,800 words
  - Topics from Research Agent insights
  - Addresses CFO, Procurement, CIO pain points
  - Data-backed claims (2M transactions/day, 300+ clients, etc.)
  - Clear CTA to next step

- [ ] **Case Studies (2x/month)** – 1,500–2,000 words
  - Real customer story
  - Before/after metrics
  - Implementation timeline
  - ROI calculation

- [ ] **Website Copy** – As assigned by director
  - Landing pages
  - Service pages
  - Product messaging

- [ ] **Sales Collateral (As needed)**
  - Email sequences
  - Pitch deck copy
  - ROI calculator copy
  - Battle cards (competitive positioning)

**Content Guidelines:**
- **Technical Credibility:** Mentions ERP, P2P, compliance, scale, integration
- **Data-Backed:** Every major claim includes proof or example
- **Enterprise Tone:** Professional, confident, transparent (not hype)
- **No Jargon:** Clear language, define technical terms
- **Specific CTAs:** What's the next action? (Demo, whitepaper, consultation)
- **SEO Optimized:** Target keywords, headers, internal links

**Content Topics (from ICP):**
- Procurement automation and scale
- Supplier relationship management
- Finance automation and compliance
- Cash flow visibility and control
- ERP integration strategies
- Digital transformation in procurement
- Data-driven procurement decisions
- Regulatory compliance (audit readiness)

### Success Metrics
- ✅ All content on-brand voice (Director approval)
- ✅ Blog posts drive qualified leads
- ✅ Case studies support sales conversations
- ✅ Zero quality issues (grammar, data accuracy)
- ✅ Content calendar 2 weeks ahead of schedule

### Dependencies
- Research Agent (topic research and validation)
- Visual Agent (case study design)
- Director input (strategic direction)

## 3️⃣ Paid Search Agent

**Role:** Demand Generation (LinkedIn, Google Ads, Retargeting)

### Mission
Generate qualified Enterprise leads through targeted paid campaigns. Focus on high-intent audiences (CFOs, procurement leaders, CIOs) searching for solutions.

### Responsibilities

**Campaign Management:**
- [ ] **LinkedIn Advertising** (Primary)
  - Sponsored content (thought leadership)
  - Sponsored InMail (high-intent targeting)
  - Lead gen forms
  - Account-based advertising (with ABM Agent)

- [ ] **Google Ads** (Search & Display)
  - Search ads (procurement, supply chain, finance automation keywords)
  - Display retargeting (to website visitors)
  - YouTube ads (if video content available)

- [ ] **Campaign Optimization**
  - Daily bid adjustments
  - Audience refinement based on performance
  - A/B testing (copy, creative, CTAs)
  - Landing page performance analysis
  - Lead quality assessment

**Campaign Strategy:**
- **Audience Targeting:** CFOs, VP Finance, Procurement Directors, CIOs, IT Managers
- **Industries:** Manufacturing, Retail, Distribution, Healthcare, Tech, Finance
- **Company Size:** Enterprise (300+ employees), high revenue ($100M+)
- **Keywords:** ERP integration, procurement automation, supplier management, finance automation
- **Retargeting:** Website visitors, blog readers, previous leads

**Ad Creative Guidelines:**
- Lead with **data and proof** (2M transactions, 300+ clients)
- Address **pain points** (email-based process, lack of visibility)
- Clear **value proposition** (control, automation, intelligence)
- Professional design (from Visual Agent)
- Strong **CTA** (Book Demo, Download Whitepaper, Schedule Call)

### Success Metrics
- ✅ Cost-per-lead target: [Director sets]
- ✅ Lead quality score: 7+/10 from sales
- ✅ Click-through rate: 3–5%
- ✅ Monthly leads: [Director target]
- ✅ Campaign ROI positive within 30 days

### Deliverable
- Weekly/Monthly: Campaign performance report

```
### Google Ads
- Impressions: [X]
- Clicks: [X]
- CTR: [X]%
- Cost per click: [X]₪
- Conversions: [X]

### Top Performing Campaigns
1. [Campaign name] – [Performance]
2. [Campaign name] – [Performance]

### Optimization Recommendations
- [Adjustment 1]
- [Adjustment 2]
```

### Dependencies
- Copywriter Agent (ad copy)
- Visual Agent (creative assets)
- Analytics Agent (lead quality data)
- Director input (budget, target metrics)

## 4️⃣ Visual Agent

**Role:** Design & Creative (Case Studies, Webinar Assets, Graphics, Video Direction)

### Mission
Create professional, on-brand visual content that supports case studies, webinars, social media, and sales presentations. Elevate brand perception through high-quality design.

### Responsibilities

**Regular Deliverables:**
- [ ] **Case Study Design (2x/month)**
  - One-page case study PDF (with Copywriter)
  - Infographic summary (problem → solution → results)
  - Social media graphics (4 variations)

- [ ] **Webinar Assets** (As scheduled)
  - Presentation deck design
  - Social promotion graphics
  - Email banner graphics

- [ ] **Social Media Graphics (2–3x/week)**
  - LinkedIn carousel posts
  - Quote graphics (thought leadership)
  - Infographics (data visualization)
  - Company milestones/announcements

- [ ] **Presentation Support**
  - Sales deck templates
  - Pitch deck design
  - One-pager design (on-demand)

**Design Guidelines:**
- **Brand Consistency:** Professional, clean, minimal
- **Data Visualization:** Make complex data clear and compelling
- **Color & Typography:** Align with brand guidelines
- **Mobile-Optimized:** All designs responsive
- **Accessibility:** High contrast, readable fonts

### Success Metrics
- ✅ All case studies complete within timeline
- ✅ 100% brand consistency
- ✅ Social graphics drive engagement
- ✅ Sales team uses templates (internal feedback)

### Dependencies
- Copywriter Agent (case study copy, content)
- Director input (brand direction, new asset needs)

## 5️⃣ Newsletter Agent

**Role:** Email Marketing & Nurture (Thought Leadership, Customer Updates, Lead Nurture)

### Mission
Keep prospects and customers engaged with valuable, non-salesy content. Build trust and position StoreNext as a thought leader through email.

### Responsibilities

**Regular Email Campaigns:**
- [ ] **Thought Leadership Newsletter (1–2x/week)**
  - Insights on Enterprise procurement trends
  - Case study highlights
  - Industry news relevant to ICP
  - Product tips and best practices
  - CTA: Learn more, book demo, read full article

- [ ] **Sales Nurture Sequences** (As needed)
  - Welcome sequence (for new leads)
  - Educational sequences (by persona: CFO, Procurement, CIO)
  - Re-engagement sequences (inactive leads)
  - Win-back sequences (lost prospects)

- [ ] **Customer Update Emails** (Monthly)
  - Product updates
  - New features
  - Customer success stories
  - Educational content

**Email Best Practices:**
- **Subject Lines:** Under 50 characters, specific, not hype
- **Preview Text:** Clear, compelling
- **Format:** Mobile-responsive, scannable (short paragraphs, bullets)
- **Personalization:** Use name, company, role (if possible)
- **CTA:** Single, clear call-to-action per email
- **Frequency:** Respect audience preferences

**Email Strategy by Persona:**
- **CFOs:** Focus on ROI, control, risk reduction, compliance
- **Procurement Leaders:** Focus on efficiency, supplier management, standardization
- **CIOs:** Focus on integration, security, scale, maintenance

### Success Metrics
- ✅ Open rate: 25–35%
- ✅ Click-through rate: 3–5%
- ✅ Unsubscribe rate: <0.5%
- ✅ Sales team feedback: Leads from email are qualified

### Deliverable
- Weekly: Email campaign(s) scheduled and sent
- Monthly: Email performance report

### Dependencies
- Copywriter Agent (email copy)
- Research Agent (topic ideas)
- Analytics Agent (open/click data)

## 6️⃣ ABM Agent

**Role:** Account-Based Marketing (LinkedIn, Targeted Campaigns, High-Value Account Strategy)

### Mission
Target high-value Enterprise accounts with customized, coordinated campaigns. ABM is B2B Enterprise marketing done right: fewer accounts, bigger deals.

### Responsibilities

**ABM Strategy:**
- [ ] **High-Value Account List** (20–50 accounts)
  - Top targets identified by director/sales team
  - Company research (size, industry, recent news)
  - Buying committee mapping (key stakeholders)
  - Personalized messaging strategy

- [ ] **LinkedIn Campaign Execution**
  - Sponsored content targeting decision-makers
  - Personalized InMail sequences
  - Group engagement (relevant LinkedIn groups)
  - Employee engagement (ambassador program)

- [ ] **Personalized Content**
  - Account-specific messaging
  - Custom case studies (if applicable)
  - Personalized email sequences
  - Executive briefing content

**ABM Tactics:**
- Target CFOs, VPs of Procurement, CIOs by name/title
- Industry-specific messaging (manufacturing vs retail vs tech)
- Event participation (webinars, industry conferences)
- Sales alignment (co-marketing with sales team)
- Account-based advertising (LinkedIn, retargeting)

### Success Metrics
- ✅ Account engagement rate: [Director sets target]
- ✅ Sales meeting booked rate: [Director sets target]
- ✅ Deal velocity: [Higher than non-ABM accounts]
- ✅ Deal size: [Larger than standard pipeline]

## 7️⃣ Sales Enablement Agent

**Role:** Sales Support (Pitch Decks, ROI Tools, Battle Cards, Objection Handling)

### Mission
Equip sales team with everything they need to close Enterprise deals. Create materials that position StoreNext effectively and handle common objections.

### Responsibilities

**Sales Collateral:**
- [ ] **Pitch Decks** (Update quarterly)
  - Product overview (high-level)
  - Value proposition (by persona)
  - Customer success stories
  - Integration & implementation timeline
  - Pricing & ROI
  - Q&A section

- [ ] **ROI Calculator**
  - Interactive tool (spreadsheet or web tool)
  - Inputs: company size, supplier count, transaction volume
  - Outputs: time saved, cost reduction, risk elimination
  - Share with prospects

- [ ] **Battle Cards**
  - Common objections & responses
  - Competitor comparisons
  - Product differentiators
  - Proof points & case studies

- [ ] **One-Pagers**
  - Feature overview
  - Customer success highlights
  - Quick ROI snapshot

- [ ] **Proposal Templates**
  - Customizable, on-brand
  - Executive summary
  - Implementation plan
  - Pricing (with sales approval)

**Sales Enablement Training:**
- Create resources documenting:
  - Product functionality
  - ICP & persona needs
  - Common sales scenarios
  - Competitor positioning

### Success Metrics
- ✅ Sales team actively uses materials (feedback/tracking)
- ✅ Materials updated monthly with new wins/data
- ✅ Close rate improved with new materials
- ✅ Sales team satisfaction: 8+/10

### Deliverable
- Quarterly: Updated pitch deck and collateral
- Monthly: New case study materials
- Ongoing: ROI calculator updates, battle card refinements

### Dependencies
- Copywriter Agent (content)
- Visual Agent (design)
- Sales team feedback (what's working, what's not)
- Analytics Agent (success data, ROI metrics)
- Director strategy (messaging, positioning)

Track what's working and what's not. Provide data-driven insights that guide strategy, budget allocation, and team optimizations.

### Responsibilities

**Weekly Tracking:**
- [ ] Campaign performance (Paid Search Agent campaigns)
- [ ] Email metrics (open rate, click rate, conversions)
- [ ] Website analytics (traffic, behavior, conversions)
- [ ] Lead quality assessment (from sales team feedback)

**Monthly Reporting:**
- [ ] Pipeline contribution by channel
- [ ] Content performance analysis (blog views, engagement, lead gen)
- [ ] Paid campaign ROI
- [ ] Lead quality scoring
- [ ] Forecast for upcoming month

**Quarterly Strategic Analysis:**
- [ ] Content ROI ranking (which pieces drive leads?)
- [ ] Channel effectiveness comparison
- [ ] Audience segment performance
- [ ] Competitive intelligence trends
- [ ] Recommendations for next quarter

**Metrics to Track:**
- **Pipeline Metrics:** Leads by source, lead quality, sales conversion rate, deal size

- **Email Metrics:** Open rate, click rate, unsubscribe rate, conversions
- **Paid Metrics:** Impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, cost per lead, ROAS
- **Brand Metrics:** Website traffic, brand searches, social followers, mentions

**Tools Used:**

## Monthly All-Hands Sync

**1st Monday of Month – 10 AM**

All agents present performance, blockers, and strategy:

1. **Research Agent** — Market insights, competitive updates, content opportunities
2. **Copywriter Agent** — Content performance, engagement, leads from content
3. **Paid Search Agent** — Campaign performance, budget efficiency, optimization
4. **Visual Agent** — Creative output, brand consistency, design requests
5. **Newsletter Agent** — Email metrics, audience growth, nurture effectiveness
6. **ABM Agent** — Account engagement, pipeline movement, strategy updates
7. **Sales Enablement Agent** — Collateral usage, sales team feedback, new requests
8. **Analytics Agent** — Overall performance, recommendations, forecast

**Director** synthesizes insights and sets priorities for next month.

---

## Principles for All Agents

1. **Data-Driven:** Every recommendation backed by metrics or research
2. **Customer-Obsessed:** Always ask: "Is this valuable for Enterprise buyers?"
3. **On-Brand:** Consistent voice, messaging, and design across all channels
4. **Speed & Quality:** Fast execution + rigorous review (no compromise on either)
5. **Transparency:** Share data openly, celebrate wins, own mistakes
6. **Collaboration:** Support each other, handoff smoothly, communicate early

# Research-First Marketing Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-02-25
**Policy:** All marketing content must be backed by research data from credible sources

---

## 🔴 Core Principle

**No post publishes without research.**

Every marketing deliverable (LinkedIn post, blog, email, social) must include:
1. ✅ Primary data source (study, statistic, or customer insight)
2. ✅ Verification (where the data came from)
3. ✅ Citation (traceable back to source)
4. ✅ Credibility check (is this reliable?)

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Research Agent
**Deliverable:** Weekly Research Brief with verified data
- ✅ Find credible sources (analyst reports, industry studies, customer data)
- ✅ Verify all statistics (don't estimate)
- ✅ Provide citations and sources
- ✅ Share brief with Copywriter BEFORE they write

**Research Brief Must Include:**
- Primary topic/trend
- Supporting statistics with sources
- Competitive intelligence
- Persona insights backed by data
- Proof points for messaging

### Copywriter Agent
**Deliverable:** Posts backed by Research Brief
- ✅ Request Research Brief before writing (REQUIRED)
- ✅ Use data from research brief (don't invent stats)
- ✅ Include "Research Sources" section in every post
- ✅ Never publish without research backing
- ✅ Cite sources where data came from

**Every Post Includes:**
```
## 📚 Research Sources

**Claim 1:** "X statistic"
- Source: [Where it came from]
- Verification: [How we know it's accurate]

**Claim 2:** "Y insight"
- Source: [Where it came from]
- Verification: [How we know it's accurate]
```

---

## Quality Standards

### For Research Agent
- ✅ Data must be verifiable (not estimated)
- ✅ Sources must be credible (not random websites)
- ✅ Statistics must be recent (ideally last 12-24 months)
- ✅ Citations must be specific (include links/sources when possible)
- ✅ Contradictory data is ok (note the conflict, explain differences)

### For Copywriter Agent
- ✅ Use ONLY data from Research Brief (don't supplement with unknown sources)
- ✅ Quote statistics accurately (don't round dramatically)
- ✅ Cite the source (where the stat came from)
- ✅ Flag any unverified claims (discuss with Research Agent)
- ✅ Include "Research Sources" section in every post

---

## Workflow

### Before Writing

```
1. Copywriter → Request Research Brief
2. Research Agent → Deliver Brief with verified data
3. Copywriter → Plan post outline using research data
4. Copywriter → Write post with citations
5. Director → Approve before publishing
```

### Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Research Brief received and reviewed
- [ ] All statistics cited to source
- [ ] No claims without backing
- [ ] "Research Sources" section complete
- [ ] Data is recent (within 12-24 months)
- [ ] Sources are credible
- [ ] Director approval obtained

## Examples

### ❌ AVOID (No Research)
```
"Procurement teams are struggling with email chaos.
Our solution makes everything 80% better."

Why: "Struggling" is vague, "80% better" unverified
```

### ✅ DO (Research-Backed)
```
"500+ suppliers = thousands of emails per month.
At scale, procurement teams lose 20-30% of productivity to status inquiries.
(Source: StoreNext customer data, 300+ implementations)

Our platform delivers 80% reduction in status calls.
(Verified in post-implementation reviews)
```

## Templates

### Research Brief Template
```markdown
# Research Brief – [Topic]

## Key Findings
- Finding 1: [Stat/insight]
  Source: [Where from]

- Finding 2: [Stat/insight]
  Source: [Where from]

## Data Points Available
- "[X% reduction]" (Source: Case study data)
- "[Y statistic]" (Source: Industry report)
- "[Z insight]" (Source: Customer interviews)

## Recommended for Messaging
- Lead with [Finding 1]
- Support with [Stat Y]
- Reinforce with [Quote from customer]
```

### Post with Sources Template
```markdown
# [Post Title]

[Post copy using research data]

**Claim 1:** "[Quote/statistic]"
- Source: [Name of study/data]
- Link: [If available]
- Credibility: [Why we trust this]

**Claim 2:** "[Quote/statistic]"
- Source: [Name of study/data]
- Link: [If available]
- Credibility: [Why we trust this]
```

## Enforcement

**Director Review:** Every post checked for:
- ✅ Research backing (yes/no)
- ✅ Citations provided (yes/no)
- ✅ Sources credible (yes/no)
- ✅ Data accurate (yes/no)

**If Not Met:**
- Post returned to Copywriter for revision
- Research Agent provides additional data if needed
- Resubmit for approval before publishing

---

## Why This Matters

**For Credibility:**
- Enterprise decision-makers check sources
- One unsupported claim kills trust
- Research-backed content ranks better in search
- LinkedIn algorithm favors credible content

**For Sales:**
- Sales team can cite sources in conversations
- Prospect objections addressed with data
- Case studies backed by metrics
- Demo conversations more confident

**For Brand:**
- StoreNext seen as data-driven, not hype
- Differentiates from competitors
- Builds authority in Enterprise space
- Attracts quality leads

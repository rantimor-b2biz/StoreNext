# LinkedIn Writing Styles — StoreNext Reference

Quick reference for the 3-style LinkedIn framework. Full skill: `T-tools/skills/linkedin-post-writer.md`

---

## The 3 Styles at a Glance

| Style | Goal | Audience | Frequency |
|-------|------|----------|-----------|
| **HubSpot** | Education + lead gen | Procurement Directors, CFOs who learn by doing | 2x/week |
| **Salesforce** | Thought leadership | CFOs and CIOs setting the strategic agenda | 1x/week |
| **Canva** | Fast engagement, reach | All personas — built for scroll-stop and sharing | 2x/week |

---

## Style 1: HubSpot

**Formula:** Pain → Insight → 3-5 steps → Open question

**Best hooks for StoreNext:**
- "The problem isn't your ERP. It's the gap between your ERP and your suppliers."
- "Stop managing supplier invoices by email. Start managing them by data."
- "65% of P2P exceptions come from the same 3 process gaps."

**Symbols:** ✓ for steps, → for flow. Max 15 words/sentence.

---

## Style 2: Salesforce

**Formula:** Big trend → Strategic insight → 3 shifts (use →) → Debate question

**Best hooks for StoreNext:**
- "The enterprises winning in 2026 aren't those with the most suppliers — they're the ones with the most supplier visibility."
- "ERP systems were built to record transactions. They weren't built to prevent them from going wrong."
- "Finance automation isn't a technology decision. It's an organizational maturity decision."

**No bullets — use → only. No em dashes. No hype.**

---

## Style 3: Canva

**Formula:** Very short hook → 3-5 lines (5-10 words each) → CTA "Comment X" or direct question

**Best hooks for StoreNext:**
- "Enterprise procurement in 2026:" (then contrast with current reality)
- "Signs your P2P process needs an upgrade:" (then relatable list)

**Max 10 words per line. Break after every thought.**

---

## Universal Post Rules

- Hook: 1-2 lines. Clear pain or surprising insight. Never "In today's post..." or "Many companies struggle..."
- Body: max 6-8 lines total. Sentences under 15 words. 3-5 bullets max.
- CTA: open question / debate invitation / minimal commitment. Never "Link in bio" or "Like and share."
- Zero em dashes. Zero hype words. No external links in caption (first comment only).
- Company/culture posts: end on a strong statement — NOT a discussion question.

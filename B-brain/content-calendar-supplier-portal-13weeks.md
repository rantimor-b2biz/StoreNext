## 📊 **CAMPAIGN METRICS**

### Weekly Targets
- **Impressions:** 50–100 per post
- **Engagements:** 5–10 per post (likes, comments, shares)
- **CTR:** 2–3%
- **Profile Visits:** +10% week-over-week

### Monthly Targets
- **Month 1 (Awareness):** 300–400 impressions, 30–40 engagements
- **Month 2 (Consideration):** 400–500 impressions, 50–70 engagements
- **Month 3 (Conversion):** 500–700 impressions, 70–100 engagements, 2–3 demo requests

### 13-Week Goals
- **Total Impressions:** 1,000–1,500
- **Total Engagements:** 150–200
- **Profile Visits Increase:** 30%–50%
- **Qualified Leads:** 2–3 per month (6–9 total)
- **Demo Requests:** 6–9
- **Pipeline Value:** $150K–$250K

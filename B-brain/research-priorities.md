## 🎯 Success Criteria

**Research is successful when:**
1. ✅ Every post backed by verified data
2. ✅ Competitive positioning is clear & defensible
3. ✅ Messaging resonates with target personas
4. ✅ Case study metrics are specific & impressive
5. ✅ Copywriter never asks "where did this data come from?"
6. ✅ Director sees credible sources in every post

---

## 📊 Output Format

Research briefs should include:
- **Key Finding** (headline)
- **Data/Proof** (specific stats or quotes)
- **Source** (where it came from, credibility)
- **Relevance** (why does this matter for messaging?)
- **Recommendation** (how should marketing use this?)

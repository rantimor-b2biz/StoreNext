# Research Brief – LinkedIn Campaign: Supplier Portal

**Date:** 2026-02-25
**Campaign:** StoreNext Supplier Portal Awareness
**Target Audience:** Procurement Leaders, VP Procurement, Procurement Directors
**Duration:** 3 months (13 weeks)

---

## Executive Summary

Procurement teams at Enterprise organizations are drowning in email-based supplier communication. They have no visibility into supplier status, no standardized process, and no way to reduce manual touchpoints.

**LinkedIn Angle:** Position StoreNext Supplier Portal as the solution that replaces email chaos with one unified, self-service portal where suppliers manage their own status, reducing 80% of manual work and freeing procurement teams to focus on strategy.

---

## Target Persona: Procurement Leader

**Profile:**
- Title: VP Procurement, Director of Procurement, Procurement Manager
- Company Size: 300–2,000+ employees
- Industry: Manufacturing, Retail, Distribution, Healthcare, Tech
- Pain Point: Managing 500–5,000 suppliers with email and spreadsheets
- Goal: Standardize procurement, improve efficiency, reduce manual work

**What They Care About:**
- Reducing supplier-related phone calls and emails
- Real-time visibility into PO and invoice status
- Standardized procurement process
- Supplier self-service (so they don't have to answer status questions)
- Compliance and documentation
- Team productivity and headcount reduction

**How They Make Decisions:**

## Primary Pain Point

> "I spend 30% of my time answering supplier calls about order and payment status. We have no standardized process. Everything is email and spreadsheets. My team is overwhelmed."

**Before StoreNext:**
- Supplier calls: "Where's my PO?" "When will I be paid?" "What's the status?"
- Manual email replies required
- No visibility into supplier commitments
- Inconsistent communication across team
- High operational overhead

**After StoreNext:**
- Suppliers log into portal, see their own status
- Zero status calls from suppliers
- Real-time visibility for procurement team
- Standardized, documented process
- 80% reduction in manual touchpoints
